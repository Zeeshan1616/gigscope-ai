# GigScope AI — Research & Evidence Methodology

## 1. Fundamental Principle

**Research first. Understand the offer second. Optimize third. Validate fourth.**

GigScope AI never rewrites drafts blindly or uses generic template formulas. Every recommendation is grounded in verified public market observations or transparently disclosed as a reasoned inference.

---

## 2. Evidence Hierarchy

All market observations, competitor findings, and keyword recommendations are strictly classified into one of four evidence tiers:

| Evidence Tier | Definition | Standard of Proof |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Directly Observed Evidence** | Information factually present in a retrieved public listing or page. | Grounded in a verified URL retrieved during the current session. |
| **Repeated Observed Pattern** | A recurring convention or deliverable observed across multiple listings. | Present in $\ge 2$ inspected candidate sources. |
| **Reasoned Inference** | A logical recommendation derived from niche taxonomy and platform guidelines. | Clearly labeled as an inference; not claimed as an empirical observation. |
| **Hypothesis / Suggestion** | A differentiation angle or underexplained scope opportunity requiring seller verification. | Flagged for seller review before publishing. |

---

## 3. Query Planning & Retrieval Strategy

Research queries are generated dynamically based on the inferred service taxonomy:

1. **Competitor Listing Search**: `site:fiverr.com "<service>"`
2. **Deliverable Standards**: `"<service>" freelance deliverables scope`
3. **Buyer Expectation Analysis**:
   - For Titles: `"<subNiche>" "<service>" top gig titles best practices`
   - For Descriptions: `"<service>" buyer expectations common pitfalls checklist`
   - For FAQs: `"<service>" buyer questions requirements before ordering`

---

## 4. Source Provenance & Disclosures

Every analysis response includes a complete `ResearchCoverage` disclosure object:
- Name of search provider used
- Count of live listings inspected
- Exact search queries executed
- Disclosed limitations and coverage gaps

### Transparency Guarantees:
- **No Fabricated Data**: We never invent competitor listings, fake reviews, or fictional pricing.
- **No Fabricated Search Volumes**: Quantitative search metrics are marked as unavailable unless backed by a dedicated keyword API.
- **No Ranking Promises**: Content quality scores are heuristic assessments, not algorithmic ranking predictions.
